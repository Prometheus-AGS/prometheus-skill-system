# Deep Research + Feynman Learning Integration

## Summary

Research into how deep research combines with Feynman learning skills, Karpathy LLM wiki patterns, and learning loop integration.

## Key Findings

- **Deep Research as Learning Primitive**: Research is fundamentally inquiry-based learning. The research process IS a learning process.
- **Feynman Loop Integration**: The explain→grade→gap→recurse loop serves as a quality gate between Report and Export stages.
- **Vertical Recursion**: Depth-first research → corresponds to drilling down on a topic until Feynman grade passes.
- **Horizontal Escalation**: Breadth-first research → corresponds to discovering new sub-topics that need explanation.
- **Auto-Generated Curriculum**: Research outputs automatically feed into `learn-plan` as curriculum DAG nodes.
- **Research as Teaching**: The best research is structured like a lesson — starts with what you know, builds to what you don't.
- **Wiki Integration**: Research results automatically populate Karpathy-style wiki entries with `index.md` + `log.md` patterns.
