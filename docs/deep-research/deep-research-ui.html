How to run the demo
  1. 1Click Example to load the sample query — a live recording of Prince's “I Love U In Me.” Attach the Prince session archive knowledge base below.
  2. 2Click Launch Research Job. A new job appears in the sidebar and opens its own workspace — report, sources, graph, and contradictions stream in live, scoped to that job.
  3. 3When it finishes, press Play on the Agent-Rendered Media Card in the job's Overview tab — an A2UI component streamed over AG-UI, playing via HLS.
