# Automation-bias countermeasures

**Change:** `change-idt-003-automation-bias-countermeasures`
**Phase:** ideation-and-decision-tools
**Goal:** 3 · **Library:** build-required

## Why

Microsoft Research (2025) found confidence in AI among the strongest
predictors of whether users engage in critical thinking at all; explainable
output increased trust while promoting over-reliance. No surveyed tool
implements any countermeasure.
