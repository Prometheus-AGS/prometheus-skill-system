## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Selected deliverables share finite phase semantics
At the completed parent implementation boundary, qualification MUST exercise the finite adopted corpus through source, generated and selected isolated installed production entry points. It MUST record actual paths, versions/content identities, observations and unsupported surfaces; matching labels or payload text alone MUST NOT certify behavior.

#### Scenario: PKG-001 source generated and isolated installation
- **WHEN** the completed source and generated Claude/Codex payloads are installed through supported repository dispatch into declared scratch roots
- **THEN** selected production entry points preserve the same scenario identity, authority and diagnostic semantics with actual observations and content identity retained

#### Scenario: PKG-001 required host unavailable
- **WHEN** an adopted harness/session or real collaborator cannot be exercised
- **THEN** the affected acceptance is BLOCKED or NOT_EXERCISED with the missing prerequisite identified, rather than substituted with payload inspection

### Requirement: Qualification preserves complete-phase and publication boundaries
Qualification MUST wait until every production change is complete, preserve material failed attempts and require confirming integration after authorized repair. It MUST report implementation, acceptance, demonstrated value and release authority separately and MUST NOT start new work automatically.

#### Scenario: PKG-001 incomplete production or missing review
- **WHEN** a production obligation remains or required independent review is pending
- **THEN** final certification remains unavailable even if a partial scenario or packaging validator passed
