# Anti-Sycophancy in Learning

Feedback must identify missing concepts and misconceptions instead of treating confidence or praise as mastery evidence. The learning skills instruct the agent to check its draft and use sycophancy-correction S-02 where available, with a documented manual fallback.

The grade persistence helper does not enforce that screening step, independently verify a score or automatically rewrite an incorrect grade. Record which checker or manual procedure ran and retain concrete corpus evidence. If the same model grades its own explanation, disclose that limitation; it is not distinct-model review.

A high score with an unresolved misconception cannot satisfy the explanation gate. Two verified transfer results and delayed retention are still required. See [Mastery Criterion](/docs/learn/mastery-criterion).

The optional [Sycophancy Correction](/docs/guide/sycophancy-correction) component provides bounded content diagnostics. Its presence or successful hook invocation does not prove that the explanation is correct or that already-written output was prevented.
